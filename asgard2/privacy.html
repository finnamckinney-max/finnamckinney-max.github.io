ASGARD 2

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 19, 2026

ASGARD 2 is a personal investment dashboard built and operated by Finn McKinney. It is used by one person, its owner, to view the owner's own Fidelity Roth IRA. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other individual.

What data is collected

ASGARD 2 connects one account — the owner's Fidelity Roth IRA — through Plaid Technologies, Inc. ("Plaid"), using Plaid's read-only Investments product. Through Plaid it receives:

  • the account's name, type and last digits;
  • holdings: securities, share quantities, prices, values and cost basis;
  • investment transactions: buys, sells, dividends, interest, fees and contributions.

It does not connect bank or credit-card accounts, and it does not request identity, account and routing numbers, or payment access. The Fidelity login is entered only into Plaid's and Fidelity's interfaces and is never seen or stored by ASGARD 2. The owner also loads statement files downloaded from Fidelity, which are treated the same way. Plaid's handling of data is described in Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own holdings, value over time, contributions and returns; to check Plaid's data against Fidelity's own statements; and to support the owner's own investment research. It is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions or profiling, and it is never sold or rented.

Services involved

  • Plaid provides the read-only connection to the account.
  • Yahoo Finance supplies public market prices and news headlines. Only ticker symbols are sent; no account information.
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes market summaries and research notes through the owner's own API account. Market summaries use public market data only. When the owner asks for analysis of their own portfolio, the holdings involved may be included. Login credentials and Plaid access tokens are never sent.

No other party receives the data.

Security

  • Runs only on the owner's personal computer and accepts connections only from that computer; it is not reachable from the internet.
  • Opening ASGARD 2 requires the owner's Windows Hello sign-in, and it locks itself when left idle.
  • The whole database is encrypted; the Plaid access token and other keys are encrypted with a key protected by Windows.
  • Linking or unlinking the account requires the owner to confirm with their PIN, and every such change is recorded in a security log.
  • Data is encrypted in transit, and related online accounts use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.

Retention and deletion

The account's records are kept as the owner's own financial history for as long as the owner uses ASGARD 2. Encrypted backups are kept on a fixed schedule — daily backups for 7 days, weekly for 4 weeks and monthly for 12 months — and older ones are deleted automatically.

When the account is unlinked, ASGARD 2 deletes its Plaid access token and asks Plaid to close the connection, so no further data is received. Records already received remain part of the owner's history unless the owner deletes them. Access can also be removed at any time through Plaid Portal.
