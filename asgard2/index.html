ASGARD 2

Personal investment dashboard

ASGARD 2 is a personal investment dashboard built and operated by Finn McKinney. It shows the owner's own Fidelity Roth IRA in one place: holdings, value over time, contributions and returns, alongside the owner's own research notes and market overview. It is the second version of ASGARD and replaces the original.

Who it is for

One person: its owner. ASGARD 2 is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not collect data from anyone else.

How the account is connected

ASGARD 2 uses Plaid's read-only Investments product to connect a single account: the owner's Fidelity Roth IRA. The Fidelity sign-in happens only in Plaid's and Fidelity's own secure windows; ASGARD 2 never sees or stores the login. It cannot move money or place trades.

How data is protected

ASGARD 2 runs only on the owner's personal computer and accepts connections only from that computer — it is not reachable from the internet. Every page needs the owner's Windows Hello sign-in, the whole database is encrypted, the Plaid access token is encrypted with a key held by Windows, and backups are encrypted. Data is never sold or shared.
