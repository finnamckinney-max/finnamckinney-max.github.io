ASGARD

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 24, 2026

ASGARD is a personal finance dashboard built and operated by Finn McKinney. It is used by one person, its owner, to view the owner's own financial accounts in one place. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from any other individual.

ASGARD was retired on November 24, 2026. It no longer runs or receives any data. Both of its Plaid connections (Discover bank and Discover credit card) were removed at Plaid that day, and the balances and transactions Plaid had supplied were deleted. Its successor, ASGARD 2, has its own privacy policy. This policy is kept as a record of how the original ASGARD handled data.

What data is collected

ASGARD uses Plaid Technologies, Inc. ("Plaid") and its Transactions product to connect the owner's own bank and credit card accounts (currently Discover). Through Plaid, ASGARD receives account names and types, balances, and transactions. The owner's login credentials are entered only into Plaid's interface and are never seen or stored by ASGARD. Plaid's handling of data is described in Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own balances, holdings, and activity, and to run the owner's own portfolio analysis. It is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, or profiling.

Sharing

The data is not sold or rented. Besides the owner, only these services are involved:

  • Plaid provides the connection to the accounts.
  • Yahoo Finance supplies public market prices and news. Only ticker symbols are sent; no account information.
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes the owner's analysis and summaries through the owner's own API account. Account figures may be included when the owner asks for analysis of their own finances. Login credentials and Plaid access tokens are never sent.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, stored on encrypted storage, and protected by unique passwords and multi-factor authentication on every related account. Only the owner has access.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as it is useful, with transactions kept for 24 months and other records on a fixed schedule. When an account is unlinked, its stored data and Plaid access are deleted. The owner can remove access at any time through ASGARD or through Plaid Portal.

Your choices

If you have questions about this policy or about data handled by ASGARD, contact asgardadmin2026@gmail.com. Data is deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its date will be posted at this address.
