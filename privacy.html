ASGARD

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 23, 2026

ASGARD is a personal investment dashboard built and operated by Finn McKinney. It is used by one person, its owner, to view the owner's own financial accounts in one place. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from any other individual.

What data is collected

When the owner links a financial account, ASGARD uses Plaid Technologies, Inc. ("Plaid") to connect to it. Through Plaid, ASGARD may receive account names and types, balances, holdings, and transactions. The owner's bank or brokerage login credentials are entered only into Plaid's interface and are never seen or stored by ASGARD. Plaid's handling of data is described in Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own balances, holdings, and activity, and to run the owner's own portfolio analysis. It is not used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions, or profiling.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone. The only party that handles it besides the owner is Plaid, which provides the connection.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, stored on encrypted storage, and protected by unique passwords and multi-factor authentication on every related account. Only the owner has access.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as it is useful, with transactions kept for 24 months and other records on a fixed schedule. When an account is unlinked, its stored data and Plaid access are deleted. The owner can remove access at any time through ASGARD or through Plaid Portal.

Your choices

If you have questions about this policy or about data handled by ASGARD, contact asgardadmin2026@gmail.com. Data is deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its date will be posted at this address.
