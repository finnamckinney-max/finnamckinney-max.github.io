ASGARD

Personal finance dashboard

ASGARD is a personal finance and investment dashboard built and operated by Finn McKinney. It brings the owner's own bank, credit card, and retirement accounts together in one place: balances, holdings, spending, and the owner's own portfolio analysis.

Who it is for

One person: its owner. ASGARD is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not collect data from anyone else.

How accounts are connected

ASGARD uses Plaid so the owner can link their own financial accounts. Bank logins are entered only into Plaid's secure window and are never seen or stored by ASGARD.

How data is protected

ASGARD runs only on the owner's personal computer and is not reachable from the internet. Opening it requires the owner's Windows Hello sign-in, the computer's drive is encrypted, and Plaid access tokens are stored encrypted. Data is never sold or shared.
