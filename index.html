ASGARD

Personal finance dashboard · Retired November 24, 2026

This app has been retired

ASGARD was replaced by ASGARD 2, a rebuilt version focused on the owner's Fidelity Roth IRA, and was retired on November 24, 2026. It no longer runs, and its Plaid connections have been removed.

ASGARD was a personal finance and investment dashboard built and operated by Finn McKinney. It brought the owner's own bank, credit card, and retirement accounts together in one place: balances, holdings, spending, and the owner's own portfolio analysis.

Who it was for

One person: its owner. ASGARD was never offered to the public, had no sign-up, and did not collect data from anyone else.

How accounts were connected

ASGARD used Plaid so the owner could link their own Discover bank and credit card accounts. Bank logins were entered only into Plaid's secure window and were never seen or stored by ASGARD. Both connections were removed at Plaid on November 24, 2026, and the data Plaid supplied was deleted. The owner's Fidelity retirement account is connected through ASGARD 2.

How data was protected

ASGARD ran only on the owner's personal computer and was not reachable from the internet. Opening it required the owner's Windows Hello sign-in, the computer's drive is encrypted, and Plaid access tokens were stored encrypted. Data was never sold or shared.
